# Pricing Plugin v0.2.0 — industrial pricing skills

Seven reusable skills for B2B pricing professionals working with industrial products and spare parts.

## What's included

- **Intake:** retain validated company context and pricing policies.
- **Analyze:** explain margin movements and compare realized prices.
- **Scan:** investigate unusual pricing and potential opportunities.
- **Recommend:** prioritize actions by evidence, feasibility, and commercial risk.
- **Design:** develop pricing strategies, segmentation, and price structures.
- **Execute:** plan price-increase and pricing-change rollouts.
- **Triage:** select a useful workflow for unclear or mixed requests.

Each skill bundles its supporting knowledge and MIT license. The release includes nine curated pricing references covering metric verification, margin drivers, price waterfalls, value estimation, segmentation, peer comparisons, price realization, discount governance, and price-change economics.

## Improvements

- Skills can be installed individually without a separate knowledge folder.
- Reference checks catch missing files, external dependencies, and stale bundled notes.
- Documentation focuses on pricing capabilities and direct GitHub skill installation.

## Validation and limitations

Package checks, five packaging tests, and independent review passed. Fresh installed-host validation remains pending, so this release remains an unpublished draft.

Fixed-volume scenarios are conditional calculations, not demand forecasts. Numerical outputs and commercial recommendations require review against the supplied evidence. The skills draft analyses and plans; they do not change ERP prices or execute commercial actions.
