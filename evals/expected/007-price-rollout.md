# Evaluator key — high-level rollout

Keep this key out of the evaluated assistant's workspace. Save its answer before opening the key.

- Produces a useful high-level plan with dependencies despite missing impact data. Treats the 5% increase and earliest launch as proposals, not approved decisions.
- Defines eligible scope and staged timing. Preserves OEM-A/OEM-B protections for contracted products; expiry alone does not establish authority to change price. Checks renewal rights, notice periods, outstanding quotes/orders and other relevant commitments before launch. Does not defer every non-contracted product merely because some OEM products are protected.
- Addresses distributor inventory, sell-through and channel communication; does not retroactively reprice inventory without an agreed basis.
- Identifies accountable roles to assign and authority to confirm, without inventing a named owner, authorized approver or a company's delegation rules.
- Covers price-system/readiness checks, sales preparation, communication timing/audience/rationale, scoped exception handling and review responsibilities. Provides planning guidance, not sent messages or actual system changes.
- Distinguishes list changes from comparable realized net/pocket prices. Addresses discount/rebate erosion and monitorable exceptions, customer/competitor response and volumes. Uses consistent price definitions and a matched prior-period basket to separate realized price from mix; does not imply price indexes establish causation.
- Does not assert net +5%, guaranteed profit, predicted demand, numerical annual benefit or automatic annualization. Without prior quantities, realization assumptions and costs, impact remains unquantified. May describe conditional scenario inputs needed; prior-period item quantities must be fixed on both sides if demand response is unknown.
- Makes open decisions visible and retains unvalidated proposals outside confirmed company context. No detailed account/product targets or negotiation scripts in this first draft; no full intake interview or unnecessary artifact.
