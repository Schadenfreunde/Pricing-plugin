# Find and assess pricing opportunities

Review this pricing export, identify unusual observations and rank the commercial opportunities. Explain the evidence behind the ranking, then layer financial scale, feasibility and customer risk onto it. Recommend proportionate next actions. Quantify supported price scenarios where possible; demand response is unknown and relevant cost data is unavailable.

Use `data.csv` and `notes.md`. The evaluation controller has seeded `confirmed-context.md` as `.pricing/context.md`; those are synthetic user-confirmed facts. Only those seeded facts count as confirmed reusable company context. New claims remain proposals.
