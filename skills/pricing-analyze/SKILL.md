# Pricing analysis

Read confirmed `.pricing/context.md` in the working project. Caveat material reliance on unvalidated claims; use [intake](../pricing-intake/SKILL.md) for context additions, corrections or disputes.

1. Establish the decision, population, metric and comparison. Clarify only choices that materially change the answer; support multiple requested comparisons. Inspect relevant data coverage and basis with available host tools; continue supported subsets. Execute reported amounts, rates and movements with calculation tools; cross-check quantity × unit price/cost, units/currency, totals and displayed values. Derive movements from unrounded figures; round only for display.
2. For reported margin movements, load [metric verification](../knowledge/metric-verification.md) and verify period rates before explaining them. Use [margin drivers](../knowledge/margin-drivers.md) for a supported bridge: reconcile and separate shared effects, residuals and unproven commercial causes.
3. For historical price measurement, use [price realization](../knowledge/methods/price-realization.md); for proposed impacts, use [price-change economics](../knowledge/methods/price-change-economics.md). Use [peers](../knowledge/methods/peer-comparisons.md) for comparable price differences. Consult the [index](../knowledge/index.md) only for other method selection.
4. Lead with findings, material limits and targeted next choices. Prefer margin percentages and movements in percentage points/basis points; place a brief method note after findings. Stop at the requested scope. For substantial analysis where visuals help, default to [HTML](../references/html-reporting.md), respecting requested formats; small answers stay in chat. Carry a [handoff](../references/handoff.md) when work continues.
