# Pricing scan

Use other pricing skills by name when they are installed and useful; they are optional. If one is unavailable, continue the supported task with these bundled references and state any material limits. For company-context updates, propose relevant facts, save only user-validated facts, and preserve unrelated existing facts and scoped exceptions. A clear direct user correction validates that fact without another approval request.

Inspect the supplied data for the user's pricing question. A broad opportunity request warrants exploration; a focused question warrants focused checks. Use available host tools and clarify only gaps that materially affect the result. Do not require a completed intake, automatically run every KPI, or substitute an opportunity scan for a small factual answer.

Read relevant `.pricing/context.md` in the working project. Reuse `pricing-intake` for proposed facts or conflicts; document claims can inform provisional reasoning with visible caveats but cannot become stored company rules without validation. Inspect the grain, price/margin definitions, currencies/units, time coverage, adjustments and matching needed for this scan. Continue supported work while flagging problems that invalidate part of it.

Select relevant references from the [knowledge index](references/knowledge/index.md). Distinguish likely data issues, unresolved structural differences, confirmed exceptions and potential commercial opportunities. Inspect both statistical patterns and meaningful combinations such as low revenue plus low margin or generous terms without corresponding commitments. Explain the observed scope, why it merits attention and the next validation. Low price or rarity is a lead, not proof of leakage.

Order commercial opportunities by confidence in the relevant evidence first. Distinguish a well-measured gap from evidence that it is commercially unjustified and correctable. Consider peer comparability, applicable policy, exceptions and capture mechanism. Use qualitative confidence with a short reason; show profitability, feasibility and customer risk as separate overlays. Do not let a large speculative amount dominate stronger evidence. Data corrections and context questions deserve their own treatment rather than collectible-upside totals.

If monetary scale is supported, use prior-period item quantities and a clearly assumed comparable realized price; explain the fixed volume and mix, period and coverage. Missing quantity, cost or realization evidence limits the estimate. An observed peer price is not an automatic target, and a revenue scenario is not guaranteed profit. The scan can remain useful without amounts.

Lead with a concise shortlist and material caveats. Offer validation/drill-down choices rather than pursuing every anomaly. Use `pricing-analyze` for deeper explanation and `pricing-recommend` when the user wants action choices. A substantial artifact may follow the [report guidance](references/html-reporting.md); adapt depth and format to the request.
