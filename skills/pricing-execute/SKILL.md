# Pricing execution planning

Turn a pricing direction into a useful first implementation plan. Establish the action, intended outcome and approval status from the request; a proposal is not an approved launch. Reuse supplied analysis or design. If the action is unclear, clarify only what changes the plan and continue supported preparation. Do not require a broad scan or full intake interview.

Read relevant `.pricing/context.md` in the working project. Apply [pricing-intake](../pricing-intake/SKILL.md) to reusable facts, conflicts or corrections; planning approval does not validate every document claim. Preserve scoped contractual exceptions. Select relevant [knowledge references](../../knowledge/index.md), especially discount governance, price realization and price-change economics.

Adapt the plan to the action and cover the material dependencies:

- Define eligible products/customers/channels and distinguish protected contracts from eligible business. Check renewal rights, notice periods, open quotes/orders and commitments. Contract expiry alone does not establish a right to reprice. Sequence by actual eligibility and readiness rather than impose one date on everyone.
- Address channel inventory, sell-through and distributor agreements where relevant. Do not assume existing stock can be retroactively repriced or a channel will pass an increase through.
- Identify accountable roles to assign for commercial approval, contract review, price-system updates, sales readiness, communication, exceptions and measurement. Confirm missing authority; suggested roles and responsibilities are proposals, not invented company mandates or named owners.
- Set high-level preparation, review and launch stages, with readiness checks and decision dependencies. Include systems/quote consistency, sales understanding of rationale and scope, communication audiences/timing, and an exception route with scope, expiry and approval to confirm. Keep communications at planning level.
- Monitor comparable realized net/pocket prices, discounts/rebates and exceptions, alongside volumes and relevant customer/competitor reactions. Define baseline, population, cadence and review responsibility as proposed choices where unknown. Watch for discount erosion; a list increase is not the same as net realization. Use the price-realization method to control basket mix, and distinguish observed outcomes from causal claims.

If impact is requested and demand response is unknown, apply [price-change economics](../../knowledge/methods/price-change-economics.md): use prior-period item quantities on both sides, explicitly holding volume/mix constant. State period, coverage, units, currency and baseline/proposed realized-price assumptions. Missing quantities or list-to-net mappings limit quantification; costs determine whether contribution can be estimated. Do not invent demand, assert guaranteed uplift or automatically annualize. A useful rollout plan can remain unquantified.

Lead with the practical plan, dependencies and decisions still needed. Match requested depth and use [report guidance](../pricing-analyze/references/html-reporting.md) only when an artifact helps. This first draft stays high-level: detailed account/product targets and negotiation scripts are deferred. Planning does not authorize price-system changes or sending customer communications.
