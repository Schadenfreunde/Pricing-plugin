# Pricing strategy and structure design

Help a pricing professional choose a commercially and operationally workable approach. Support both a new strategy and redesign of an existing structure. Understand the objective and decision before choosing methods; a useful first direction can be qualitative and should not require transaction data or a full intake interview.

Read relevant `.pricing/context.md` in the working project. Reuse [pricing-intake](../pricing-intake/SKILL.md) for proposed facts, corrections or conflicts. Use supplied document claims provisionally with caveats where material; proposed structures and estimated benefits are not automatically confirmed company rules. Preserve the scope of existing contracts and tolerated exceptions.

For a new strategy, identify the offer, relevant buyers/use cases, credible next-best alternatives, differentiated value, seller economics, competition, adoption constraints and operational feasibility as needed. Select relevant references from the [knowledge index](../../knowledge/index.md), especially value estimation and segmentation. Distinguish economic value from willingness to pay and captured price. Missing competitor quotes or customer-value inputs constrain numerical recommendations; avoid an invented optimal price, uniform value-capture percentage or demand response.

For redesign, first understand the current structure and problem. Consider how list/discount logic, service/channel/region differences, customer/product segmentation, governance, system limitations and transition affect the decision. Historical discount medians or peer prices do not alone define sound future policy; differing list bases can invalidate discount comparisons. Prefer intelligible, enforceable differentiation over complexity that the sales process cannot use.

Compare a small number of viable alternatives and recommend a direction with reasons, assumptions, tradeoffs and the next evidence needed. Illustrative price/structure examples should be explicitly scoped assumptions rather than invented company targets. Use [pricing-analyze](../pricing-analyze/SKILL.md) for relevant evidence gaps and [pricing-recommend](../pricing-recommend/SKILL.md) when choosing a pricing action from analysis; do not impose a workflow chain.

For an existing structure, include a high-level transition/validation path respecting contract timing, exception scope, operating feasibility and comparable realized outcomes. Do not invent implementation authority, detailed account targets or an approved rollout. Match the user's requested depth and format; use [report guidance](../pricing-analyze/references/html-reporting.md) only when an artifact helps. Lead with the practical design direction and material qualifications.
