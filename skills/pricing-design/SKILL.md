# Pricing design

Read confirmed `.pricing/context.md` in the working project. Caveat material reliance on unvalidated claims; use [intake](../pricing-intake/SKILL.md) for context changes.

1. Establish the objective. For a **new strategy**, identify the offer, buyers/use cases, credible alternatives, differentiated value, seller economics, competition and adoption/operating constraints. Use [value estimation](../knowledge/methods/value-estimation.md) and [segmentation](../knowledge/methods/segmentation.md).
2. For **redesign**, understand the current problem, list/discount logic, service/channel/region differences, segments, governance and system limits. Use [segmentation](../knowledge/methods/segmentation.md) and [governance](../knowledge/methods/discount-governance.md) as needed. Historical medians and differing list-base discounts do not define sound future policy.
3. Compare a few workable structures and choose a direction: price logic, meaningful eligibility/fences, relevant controls, rationale and tradeoffs. Qualitative direction is useful without complete data. Numerical examples require explicit supported assumptions; avoid invented targets, willingness to pay, value-capture percentages or demand response. Use [analysis](../pricing-analyze/SKILL.md) for decision-changing evidence gaps.
4. State high-level transition/validation constraints, preserving contracts and exceptions. Detailed rollout stages, roles and readiness belong to [execute](../pricing-execute/SKILL.md) when requested. Lead with the design choice and material qualifications; use [report guidance](../references/html-reporting.md) when helpful and a [handoff](../references/handoff.md) for continued work.
