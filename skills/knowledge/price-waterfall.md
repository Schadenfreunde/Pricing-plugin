# Read a company-defined price waterfall

A waterfall follows signed adjustments from a starting price to later levels. Illustrative stages include list price, invoice discounts/surcharges, invoice price, off-invoice rebates and net price. Pocket price may include non-cash costs and cost of money. Use the company's validated levels, signs and mappings; include only relevant supported steps.

Compare the exact price level on a comparable scope, period, currency/unit and adjustment basis. Reconcile each step under those definitions. Locate credits, returns, accruals, late rebates, freight, services and financing only where supported; expose unknown/unmatched amounts. Require list/adjustment evidence before attributing a lower realized price to increased discounting.

For policy applicability, authority or scoped exceptions, use [discount governance](methods/discount-governance.md).

Pocket margin and gross margin can have different revenue adjustments, cost boundaries and denominators. Reconcile their definitions before connecting a waterfall to a gross-margin movement. With no adjustment records, a waterfall may add no supported explanation.
