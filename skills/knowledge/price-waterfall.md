# Read a company-defined price waterfall

## Define the levels

- A waterfall follows signed adjustments from a starting price to later levels.
- Illustrative stages: list price, invoice discounts and surcharges, invoice price, off-invoice rebates, net price.
- Pocket price may also include non-cash costs and cost of money.
- Use the company's validated levels, signs and mappings. Include only relevant, supported steps.
- For actor and value-basis choices (funding, fees, settlements, sell-through), use [retail and ecommerce economics](retail-economics.md).
- For policy applicability, authority or scoped exceptions, use [discount governance](methods/discount-governance.md).

## Compare and reconcile

- Compare the exact price level on a comparable scope, period, currency, unit and adjustment basis.
- Reconcile each step under those definitions.
- Place credits, returns, accruals, late rebates, freight, services and financing only where supported.
- Expose unknown or unmatched amounts.
- Require list and adjustment evidence before attributing a lower realized price to increased discounting.

## Connect to gross margin

- Pocket margin and gross margin can differ in revenue adjustments, cost boundaries and denominators.
- Reconcile their definitions before linking a waterfall to a gross-margin movement.
- With no adjustment records, a waterfall may add no supported explanation.
