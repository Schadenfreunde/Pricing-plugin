# Design the value unit, package and charging model

Use when choosing how an offer creates value, becomes purchasable and is charged. Reuse [value estimation](value-estimation.md) and [segmentation](segmentation.md) as their canonical methods for buyer value and enforceable fences.

## Define the structure

Establish buyer/user, use case, credible alternative, seller economics and adoption constraints. Distinguish the customer's value unit, the priced unit, the entitlement and the billing cadence.

Compare relevant structures:
- One-time purchase, recurring access/service, measured usage, outcome payment or a hybrid.
- Packages/features, included usage or service, overages, minimums, commitments, add-ons and upgrade/downgrade rights.
- Monthly/annual billing and contract term; upfront collection and service duration are separate choices.
- Trial, free tier, entry package or paid onboarding, where useful for adoption and economics.

Evaluate value alignment, predictability, cost exposure, measurability, customer understanding, enforceability, channel fit and operational feasibility. Usage or outcome pricing transfers risks; establish who controls the measure and how it is verified. Metering feasibility does not establish willingness to pay.

## Compare alternatives

Use a few meaningful options rather than a universal template. Explain each option's eligibility/fences, capture mechanism, customer consequences and seller economics. Consumer packages may use benefits, convenience or access where financial buyer value cannot be defensibly quantified.

Trials/free tiers need a target user, value-discovery path, conversion/upgrade mechanism and supported cost/abuse assumptions. Annual discounts need a reason and an economics/retention comparison; no fixed percentage or trial length is a default.

State numerical assumptions and horizons. Fixed fees, commitments and included usage may change outcomes nonlinearly; use [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) instead of averaging unlike units. Qualitative direction remains useful with incomplete data.

Check arbitrage, cannibalization, package migration, entitlement consistency, fairness and applicable contract/channel constraints. Use [hybrid offers](hybrid-offers.md) for physical/service bundles and [renewals and migration](renewals-and-migration.md) for existing customers. A structure choice remains proposed until commercially approved; detailed rollout belongs to the execution workflow.
