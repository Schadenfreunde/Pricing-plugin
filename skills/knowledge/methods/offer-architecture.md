# Design the value unit, package and charging model

- Use when choosing how an offer creates value, becomes purchasable and is charged.
- Reuse [value estimation](value-estimation.md) and [segmentation](segmentation.md) as the canonical methods for buyer value and enforceable fences.

## Define the structure

- Establish the buyer or user, use case, credible alternative, seller economics and adoption constraints.
- Distinguish the customer's value unit, the priced unit, the entitlement and the billing cadence.
- Compare relevant structures:
  - one-time purchase, recurring access or service, measured usage, outcome payment, or a hybrid
  - packages and features, included usage or service, overages, minimums, commitments, add-ons, and upgrade or downgrade rights
  - monthly or annual billing and contract term; upfront collection and service duration are separate choices
  - trial, free tier, entry package or paid onboarding, where useful for adoption and economics
- Evaluate value alignment, predictability, cost exposure, measurability, customer understanding, enforceability, channel fit and operational feasibility.
- Usage or outcome pricing transfers risk. Establish who controls the measure and how it is verified.
- Metering feasibility does not establish willingness to pay.

## Compare alternatives

- Use a few meaningful options, not a universal template.
- For each option, explain eligibility and fences, capture mechanism, customer consequences and seller economics.
- Consumer packages may use benefits, convenience or access where financial buyer value cannot be defensibly quantified.

**Trials, free tiers and annual discounts**

- Trials and free tiers need a target user, a value-discovery path, a conversion or upgrade mechanism, and supported cost and abuse assumptions.
- Annual discounts need a reason and an economics and retention comparison.
- No fixed discount percentage or trial length is a default.

**Numbers**

- State numerical assumptions and horizons.
- Fixed fees, commitments and included usage can change outcomes nonlinearly. Use [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) instead of averaging unlike units.

## Check before proposing

- Check arbitrage, cannibalization, package migration, entitlement consistency, fairness and applicable contract or channel constraints.
- Use [hybrid offers](hybrid-offers.md) for physical and service bundles.
- Use [renewals and migration](renewals-and-migration.md) for existing customers.
- Detailed rollout belongs to the execution workflow.
