# Plan renewals and migration of pricing or packages

Use for changes affecting existing recurring customers, legacy packages, commitments or entitlements. Reuse [discount governance](discount-governance.md) for authority/exception decisions and [recurring metrics](../recurring-metrics.md) for measurement.

## Establish eligible cohorts

Map the current offer/price/package, applicable contract and service period, renewal/effective dates, notice requirements, commitments, cancellation/downgrade rights and confirmed exceptions. Expiry or a status label does not establish a right to reprice. Separate new-business offers from protected, eligible and unresolved existing cohorts.

Identify target-package mapping, entitlement continuity, included usage, credits/balances and supported proration/refund rules. Stable account/item lineage matters when replacement subscription IDs could otherwise appear as churn and new acquisition. Keep unresolved legal/commercial terms as decisions for the responsible owner.

## Compare transition choices

Consider immediate changes where permitted, renewal-only transitions, staged increases, time-bounded grandfathering, package migration or opt-in alternatives. Compare customer value, seller economics, fairness, administration, forecast coverage and operating feasibility. Historical discount habits do not create approval for new concessions.

Define what grandfathering protects, who qualifies, its duration and treatment of upgrades, add-ons or ownership changes. A phase-in needs effective dates and an approved scope; it is not a universal recommendation.

Use [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) for conditional economics over the actual renewal horizon. Separate scheduled eligibility from assumed acceptance, collections, churn and realized outcomes.

## Prepare and measure

For execution planning, identify roles and readiness for contract review, commercial approval, catalog/rating/entitlement consistency, invoice preview, customer communication, exceptions, support and recovery. Hold only affected launch cohorts until their dependencies are resolved. Successful technical previews do not grant commercial authority.

Set a baseline, cohort/window, method/version and review owner. Monitor realized recurring contribution, price/package movements, retention, concessions, payment/refund issues and entitlement/service outcomes as relevant. Compare mature cohorts and retain original versus migrated definitions where needed.

Keep the plan proportionate and high-level unless detailed migration work is requested. Plans do not authorize billing updates, feature changes, outbound messages or production migrations. Report chosen versus approved status and unresolved dependencies.
