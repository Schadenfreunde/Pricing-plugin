# Plan renewals and migration of pricing or packages

- Use for changes affecting existing recurring customers, legacy packages, commitments or entitlements.
- Reuse [discount governance](discount-governance.md) for authority and exception decisions.
- Reuse [recurring metrics](../recurring-metrics.md) for measurement.

## Establish eligible cohorts

- Map, for each cohort:
  - current offer, price and package
  - applicable contract and service period
  - renewal and effective dates, and notice requirements
  - commitments, and cancellation or downgrade rights
  - confirmed exceptions
- Expiry or a status label does not establish a right to reprice.
- Separate new-business offers from protected, eligible and unresolved existing cohorts.
- Identify target-package mapping, entitlement continuity, included usage, credits and balances, and supported proration and refund rules.
- Keep stable account and item lineage, so replacement subscription IDs do not appear as churn plus new acquisition.
- Keep unresolved legal or commercial terms as decisions for the responsible owner.

## Compare transition choices

- Consider: immediate changes where permitted, renewal-only transitions, staged increases, time-bounded grandfathering, package migration, or opt-in alternatives.
- Compare customer value, seller economics, fairness, administration, forecast coverage and operating feasibility.
- Historical discount habits do not create approval for new concessions.
- Grandfathering: define what it protects, who qualifies, its duration and how upgrades, add-ons or ownership changes are treated.
- A phase-in needs effective dates and an approved scope. It is not a universal recommendation.
- Use [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) for conditional economics over the actual renewal horizon.
- Separate scheduled eligibility from assumed acceptance, collections, churn and realized outcomes.

## Prepare and measure

**Readiness**

- Identify roles and readiness for contract review, commercial approval, catalog, rating and entitlement consistency, invoice preview, customer communication, exceptions, support and recovery.
- Hold only the affected launch cohorts until their dependencies are resolved.
- Successful technical previews do not grant commercial authority.

**Measurement**

- Set a baseline, cohort and window, method and version, and review owner.
- Monitor as relevant: realized recurring contribution, price and package movements, retention, concessions, payment and refund issues, and entitlement and service outcomes.
- Compare mature cohorts. Keep original versus migrated definitions where needed.

**Scope of the plan**

- Keep the plan proportionate and high-level unless detailed migration work is requested.
- Report chosen versus approved status and unresolved dependencies.
