# Price hybrid physical, software and service offers

- Use for asset-plus-software or service bundles, physical subscriptions, rentals and usage or outcome offers.
- Establish the commercial actor, asset ownership, customer and use case, and entitlement.
- Use [offer architecture](offer-architecture.md) for structure choices.
- Apply [metric verification](../metric-verification.md) to the requested measures.

## Establish the economic units

**Components and populations**

- Separate physical sale or rental, recurring access or service, included consumables, measured usage and outcome payments.
- Name the eligible installed base and attachment denominator. Sold, installed, active, technically capable and contractually eligible assets are different populations.
- Link assets or sites, contracts or accounts, and entitlement items without duplicating revenue.

**Physical subscriptions**

- Connect deliveries, skipped or paused cycles, fulfillment, returns, recovery and retained subscription state under the supplied skip-charge policy.
- A shipment and a recurring entitlement need not share a grain or effective date.

**Costs**

- Identify supported costs: hardware acquisition and recovery, consumables, logistics, maintenance, support, hosting, payment, warranty, failure and replacement.
- Distinguish accounting cost, cash investment and relevant contribution.
- Do not charge depreciation and capital recovery twice in the same view.
- Use supplied finance treatment for recognized revenue and allocation. Do not fabricate a bundle allocation from standalone list prices.

## Choose and compare structures

- Compare relevant structures: purchase plus service, subscription or rental, fixed plus usage, commitment and outcome.
- Match the value unit to utilization, value and cost exposure.
- Asset life, idle periods, failure and downtime, connectivity and service capacity affect economics. Do not assume constant utilization or zero delivery cost.

**Metered and outcome offers**

- Need an observable agreed measure, data ownership, a verification and dispute process, and explicit control of performance risk.
- Technical telemetry does not prove reliable billing or the customer's willingness to pay.

**Comparing economics**

- Show one-time and recurring contribution separately, plus a consistent finite-horizon combined view where supported.
- Compare cash and payback under declared timing assumptions.
- An attach-rate opportunity is sized on the eligible installed base, not all historical equipment sales, and needs a feasible adoption or capture mechanism.

## Interpret and transition

- Use [retail economics](../retail-economics.md) for fulfillment and channel effects.
- Use [recurring metrics](../recurring-metrics.md) for the recurring portion and [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) for schedules and response assumptions.
- Assess package, contract and asset lineage and entitlement continuity with [renewals and migration](renewals-and-migration.md).
- Report missing service history, asset eligibility, skip, refund or recovery policy, or cost evidence beside the affected conclusion. Hold only the affected economics.
- Commercial approvals, contract review and operational readiness remain necessary for affected rollout cohorts.
