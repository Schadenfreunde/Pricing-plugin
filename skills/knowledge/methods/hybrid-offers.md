# Price hybrid physical, software and service offers

Use for asset-plus-software/service bundles, physical subscriptions, rentals and usage/outcome offers. Establish the commercial actor, asset ownership, customer/use case, entitlement and charging components. Use [offer architecture](offer-architecture.md) for structure choices and apply [metric verification](../metric-verification.md) to the requested measures.

## Establish the economic units

Separate physical sale/rental, recurring access/service, included consumables, measured usage and outcome payments. Name the eligible installed base and attachment denominator: sold, installed, active, technically capable and contractually eligible assets are different populations. Link assets/sites, contracts/accounts and entitlement items without duplicating revenue.

For physical subscriptions, connect deliveries, skipped/paused cycles, fulfillment, returns, recovery and retained subscription state under the supplied skip-charge policy. A shipment and a recurring entitlement need not share a grain or effective date.

Identify supported hardware acquisition/recovery, consumables, logistics, maintenance, support, hosting, payment, warranty and failure/replacement costs. Distinguish accounting cost, cash investment and relevant contribution; depreciation and capital recovery cannot be charged twice in the same view. Use supplied finance treatment for recognized revenue/allocation; do not fabricate bundle allocation from standalone list prices.

## Choose and compare structures

Compare relevant purchase-plus-service, subscription/rental, fixed-plus-usage, commitment and outcome structures. Match value unit to utilization/value and cost exposure. Asset life, idle periods, failure/downtime, connectivity and service capacity affect economics; do not assume constant utilization or zero delivery cost.

Metered/outcome offers need an observable agreed measure, data ownership, verification/dispute process and explicit control of performance risk. Technical telemetry does not prove reliable billing or the customer's willingness to pay.

Show one-time and recurring contribution separately and a consistent finite-horizon combined view where supported. Compare cash/payback under declared timing assumptions. An attach-rate opportunity requires an eligible population and feasible adoption/capture mechanism, not all historical equipment sales.

## Interpret and transition

Use [retail economics](../retail-economics.md) for fulfillment/channel effects, [recurring metrics](../recurring-metrics.md) for the recurring portion and [recurring scenarios](recurring-scenarios.md) for schedules and response assumptions. Assess package/contract/asset lineage and entitlement continuity with [renewals and migration](renewals-and-migration.md).

Keep forecast adoption, utilization and retention separate from observed results. Report missing service history, asset eligibility, skip/refund/recovery policy or cost evidence beside the affected conclusion; hold only affected economics. Commercial approvals, contract review and operational readiness remain necessary for affected rollout cohorts.
