# Retail and ecommerce economics

Use for manufacturer/channel, retailer, marketplace or direct-consumer economics. Establish whether the view is from a manufacturer, retailer/direct seller, marketplace platform or marketplace merchant, and whether records represent shipments, consumer purchases or settlements. Manufacturer sell-in does not establish consumer demand or retail pass-through.

Apply [metric verification](metric-verification.md) to the selected revenue, cost and adjustment basis. Compare like-for-like SKU/variant, pack, location/channel, period and currency; pack changes, stockouts and availability can alter the basket. GMV, merchant revenue, settled merchant proceeds and platform revenue are distinct measures; name the selected actor and measure. A payout settles economic events, not a second sale.

## Reconcile the economic view

Start from the confirmed price/revenue level and include each supported adjustment once: discounts, coupons, rebates, trade funding, returns, credits, tax and delivery treatment. Distinguish retailer-funded discounts from supplier reimbursements, cash settlement from an economic event, and a refund request from a completed payment transaction. A net source amount may already contain an adjustment.

State the cost boundary. Product gross margin, order contribution and pocket margin need different recipes. For contribution, include applicable merchandise, fulfillment, payment/store/marketplace fees, return handling and other attributable variable costs on the stated basis. Show acquisition spend or allocated overhead separately unless included in the confirmed measure. Returned stock recovery and unrecovered fees follow supplied treatment; missing costs remain unavailable.

For manufacturer RGM, reconcile invoice adjustments, accruals, promotion funding and cost to serve. Use supported sell-through and funding/settlement evidence to assess channel outcomes.

For AOV, declare the revenue basis and divide by distinct qualifying order IDs under a stated cancelled/refunded eligibility policy; count repeat orders separately. ARPU/ARPA use users/accounts instead.

## Interpret changes and choices

Separate comparable price movement from SKU/pack/channel mix, quantities, availability and cost changes. Use [price realization](methods/price-realization.md) only for a comparable linear unit-price basket, with prior-period quantities held fixed.

A promotion can raise sales while lowering contribution; compare scenarios on one economic basis and state the objective (acquisition, markdown, clearance) rather than inventing a universal floor or optimum. Use [experiments and promotions](methods/experiments-and-promotions.md) for funding, cannibalization, stacking and incremental-effect evidence: historical price-sales association, short-term lift or a low margin alone does not establish causality or collectible upside. Recurring deliveries additionally require [recurring metrics](recurring-metrics.md) and [hybrid offers](methods/hybrid-offers.md) when relevant.
