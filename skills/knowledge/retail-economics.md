# Retail and ecommerce economics

- Use for manufacturer and channel, retailer, marketplace or direct-to-consumer economics.
- Establish whose view it is: manufacturer, retailer or direct seller, marketplace platform, or marketplace merchant.
- Establish what records represent: shipments, consumer purchases or settlements.
- Manufacturer sell-in does not establish consumer demand or retail pass-through.

## Choose the measure

- Apply [metric verification](metric-verification.md) to the selected revenue, cost and adjustment basis.
- Compare like-for-like SKU or variant, pack, location or channel, period and currency.
- Pack changes, stockouts and availability can alter the basket.
- GMV, merchant revenue, settled merchant proceeds and platform revenue are distinct. Name the selected measure.

## Reconcile the economic view

**Revenue and adjustments**

- Start from the confirmed price or revenue level.
- Include each supported adjustment once: discounts, coupons, rebates, trade funding, returns, credits, tax and delivery.
- Distinguish:
  - retailer-funded discounts from supplier reimbursements
  - cash settlement or payout from the economic events it settles; a payout is not a second sale
  - a refund request from a completed payment transaction
- A net source amount may already contain an adjustment.

**Cost boundary**

- State the cost boundary. Product gross margin, order contribution and pocket margin need different recipes.
- For contribution, include applicable merchandise, fulfillment, payment, store or marketplace fees, return handling and other attributable variable costs on the stated basis.
- Show acquisition spend or allocated overhead separately, unless the confirmed measure includes them.
- Returned-stock recovery and unrecovered fees follow supplied treatment.

**Manufacturer revenue growth management (RGM)**

- Reconcile invoice adjustments, accruals, promotion funding and cost to serve.
- Assess channel outcomes with supported sell-through and funding or settlement evidence.

**Order and customer averages**

- AOV: declare the revenue basis and divide by distinct qualifying order IDs under a stated cancelled or refunded eligibility policy.
- Count repeat orders separately.
- ARPU and ARPA divide by users or accounts instead.

## Interpret changes and choices

- Separate comparable price movement from SKU, pack or channel mix, quantities, availability and cost changes.
- Use [price realization](methods/price-realization.md) only for a comparable linear unit-price basket, with prior-period quantities held fixed.
- A promotion can raise sales while lowering contribution.
- Compare scenarios on one economic basis and state the objective (acquisition, markdown, clearance). Do not invent a universal floor or optimum.
- Use [experiments and promotions](methods/experiments-and-promotions.md) for funding, cannibalization, stacking and incremental-effect evidence.
- Historical price-sales association, short-term lift or a low margin alone does not establish causality or collectible upside.
- Recurring deliveries also need [recurring metrics](recurring-metrics.md), and [hybrid offers](methods/hybrid-offers.md) when relevant.
